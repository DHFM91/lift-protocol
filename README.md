# Lift Protocol

An elevator-action infiltration game in a single HTML file, inspired by Taito's 1983 arcade classic *Elevator Action*.

**Play:** https://dhfm91.github.io/lift-protocol/

Drop onto the roof of a procedurally generated tower and work your way down. Grab the secret files behind every red door, then escape in the getaway car on the ground floor. Taking out hostiles is optional: it adds to your score, and wiping out the whole building earns a clean-sweep bonus. Each new building is taller, with more lifts, more enemies and tougher enemy types.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | ← → or A D |
| Duck | ↓ |
| Jump | Z or Space |
| Fire | X or J (3 shots on screen) |
| Stairs | Stand at a building edge, hold ↑ / ↓ |
| Lift | Step inside a stopped car, hold ↑ / ↓ |
| Red door | Stand in front, press ↑ |
| Pause / sound | P / M |

When the game ends, a qualifying run goes on the global top 10 (initials, score and the building reached).

Touch buttons appear on phones and tablets.

## Tricks

- Duck under high shots, jump over low ones.
- Jump into an enemy to kick them.
- Jump and fire at a ceiling lamp to drop it on whoever is below and black out the floor.
- Lifts are weapons: lower a car onto anyone standing in the shaft pit, or carry someone on its roof up into the shaft ceiling.
- Ride on top of a car (but get off before it reaches the top), or jump across an open shaft.

Take too long and the alarm sounds: enemies fire faster, hunt you across floors and call in reinforcements.
