# Lift Protocol

An elevator-action infiltration game in a single HTML file, inspired by Taito's 1983 arcade classic *Elevator Action*.

**Play:** https://dhfm91.github.io/lift-protocol/

Drop onto the roof of a procedurally generated tower and work your way down. Clear every hostile in the building, grab the secret files behind the red doors, then escape in the getaway car on the ground floor. Each new building is taller, with more lifts, more enemies and tougher enemy types.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | ← → or A D |
| Duck | ↓ |
| Jump | Z or Space |
| Fire | X or J (3 shots on screen) |
| Stairs | Stand at a building edge, hold ↑ / ↓ |
| Lift | Step inside a stopped car, hold ↑ / ↓ |
| Red door | Stand in front, press ↑ |
| Pause / sound | P / M |

Touch buttons appear on phones and tablets.

## Tricks

- Duck under high shots, jump over low ones.
- Jump into an enemy to kick them.
- Jump and fire at a ceiling lamp to drop it on whoever is below and black out the floor.
- Lower a lift car onto anyone standing in the shaft pit.
- Ride on top of a car, or jump across an open shaft.

Take too long and the alarm sounds: enemies fire faster, hunt you across floors and call in reinforcements.
