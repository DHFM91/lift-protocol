LIFT PROTOCOL

Infiltrate the tower from the roof, ride the lifts, crack every red door for the secret files, then get in the getaway car parked on the ground floor.
Move ← → or A D
Duck ↓   Jump Z / Space
Fire click or X / J (3 shots on screen)
Stairs stand at a building edge, hold ↑ / ↓
Lift step inside a stopped car, hold ↑ / ↓
Red door stand in front, ↑ to grab the file
Blue door ↑ to hide inside, ↓ to step out; a green ✓ means it's cleared (no more spawns, still a hiding spot)
Getaway car once every file is taken, stand by the car and ↑ to get in
Tricks duck under high shots, jump low ones; jump into enemies to kick; jump-fire at a lamp to drop it; lower a car onto anyone in the pit, or carry them on its roof into the shaft ceiling.
